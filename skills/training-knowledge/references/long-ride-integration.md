# Long Ride Integration

## Pre-long-ride activation

Triggered before rides longer than ~2.5–3 hours. 5–7 minutes total.

- Dead bug with long exhale
- Wall-supported thoracic extensions
- Scap push-ups

## Post-long-ride durability reset

Performed after the longest weekly ride. 8–10 minutes. Not optional during endurance blocks.

- Hip flexor isometric stretch
- Long-lever glute bridge holds
- Prone Y–T–W holds

## Cardiac decoupling (durability benchmark)

Tracked in Intervals.icu after long rides.

- Under 5%: excellent
- 5–10%: acceptable, monitor
- Above 10%: concern — reduce duration next long ride, do not add volume

## Durability context

Durability is the current training constraint — legs and cardio are ahead of time-in-saddle capacity. The limiter is the body beyond 3–4 hours. Progressive long ride duration increases are the primary adaptation target through May 3.
