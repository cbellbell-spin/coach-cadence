# Route Library

| Route | Distance | Gain | Duration | Purpose |
|-------|----------|------|----------|---------|
| Tunnel Road Loop | ~24.5 mi | ~2,507 ft | ~1h50m–2h | Primary intensity route; one sustained climb to ~1,488ft peak; IF ~0.75 typical; hilly terrain drives power spikes |
| Pinehurst 3000 | ~30 mi | ~3,000 ft | ~2h15m | Moderate volume; rolling/punchy climbs; wild temp swings, can get cold in canyon; not ideal for sustained threshold work |
| Bridge | ~20 mi | ~1,000 ft | ~90 min | Best Z2 option; wind on bridge can be rough |
| Bridge family | Modular | +250 ft/loop | +~30 min/loop | Each extra loop adds ~8 mi, 250 ft, ~30 min; "+ section" adds ~5 mi, 500 ft once at end (doesn't stack); wind on bridge can be rough |
| Coyote Creek corridor | Variable | ~1,234 ft | ~4h+ | 100% paved; gradual climb southbound; Z2 execution without forced HR spikes; turnaround at Morgan Hill Starbucks |

## Route selection guidelines

- Z2 long ride: Bridge family (modular duration) or Coyote Creek (clean Z2, no forced spikes)
- Intensity session: Tunnel Road Loop
- When cold or variable weather: avoid Pinehurst canyon
- Flag route choices that unnecessarily force higher zones — terrain-forced spikes are acceptable, chosen intensity is not
