# Strength Training Template

**Purpose:** Preserve and build muscle mass, support cycling, protect spine and joints, improve longevity. Strength is supportive — no PR chasing.

---

## Weekly structure

- Frequency: 2 sessions/week
- Session length: 30–45 minutes
- Rest days between sessions: preferred, not mandatory
- Never train to failure
- If time is tight: shorten sets, do not skip sessions

---

## Session A: Lower Body + Trunk (Sciatica-aware)

**Warm-up (5 min):** Hip hinge patterning, glute activation, gentle spinal mobility

**Main work:**
1. Step-ups — 2–3 × 6–10/side, controlled descent, upright torso
2. Romanian deadlift — 2–3 × 6–10, moderate load, neutral spine, stop if form degrades
3. Leg Press — 2–3 × 6–8
4. Glute bridge / hip thrust — 2–3 × 8–12, pause at top
5. Leg Curl — 2–3 × 8–12 controlled
6. Calf Raises — 2–3 × 12–20

**Trunk:**
7. Dead bug or Standing Pallof press — 2–3 × 6–10 reps or 20–30 sec holds
8. Side plank — 2 × 20–40 sec/side

**Durability Add-on A** (after main work, no load progression):
9. Step-downs slow eccentric — 2–3 × 5/side
10. Half-kneeling Pallof press — 2 × 8/side
11. Tibialis Raise — 2 × 20

---

## Session B: Upper Body + Trunk

**Warm-up (5 min):** Band pull-aparts, shoulder mobility

**Main work:**
1. Push (DB bench or push-ups) — 2–3 × 6–10
2. Pull (row or lat pulldown) — 2–3 × 8–12
3. Overhead press, light to moderate — 2–3 × 6–10, no spinal bracing fatigue

**Accessory:**
4. Face pulls or band pull-aparts — 2 × 12–15

**Trunk:**
5. Anti-rotation or carry — 2–3 × 20–40 sec

**Durability Add-on B** (postural endurance, after main work):
6. Chin tuck and lift supine — 5 × 10 sec holds
7. Prone Y–T–W holds — 2 rounds, 20–30 sec/position
8. Wrist extension isometrics — 3 × 30 sec

---

## Load and progression rules

- Finish each set with 1–2 reps in reserve
- Increase load only when: all reps are clean AND no back or nerve symptoms
- Unilateral lifts progressed using weaker side
- Progression options: slight load increase, one extra set, better control/tempo
- Soreness is not a success metric

---

## Integration with cycling

- Never schedule heavy lower body strength within 48 hours of intensity riding
- MTB intensity weeks: reduce lower body loads
- If legs feel dead: reduce load, keep movement patterns
- Skipping strength is a last resort

---

## Red flags — stop or regress immediately

- Sciatic symptoms
- Loss of spinal control
- Grinding reps
- Excess soreness impacting sleep or riding

---

## Weekly anchors

- Session A: Monday (lower body + trunk)
- Session B: Friday (upper body + trunk)
- Session B must not be skipped to accommodate rides
